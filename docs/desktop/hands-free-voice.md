## Conversation controls

Voice mode offers Hands-free and Tap to talk. Starting hands-free capture keeps the chosen microphone active while the voice pane is open. Speech detection keeps a short leading buffer, sends a turn after the selected silence interval, and limits each utterance to under two minutes. Silence and brief clicks do not submit requests. Dictation outside voice mode still inserts a transcript into the composer for review.

The pane shows microphone activity, listening, local transcription, request submission, thinking, and speech playback. Speaking during a reply stops playback and captures the new turn. Speaking while inference is running cancels that request, preserves its partial response, and waits for cancellation before submitting another turn. Pausing or ending voice stops microphone tracks and pending voice work. Opening another settings/workspace overlay also stops capture. A tool review closes voice mode so its written approval remains available without cancelling the reviewed operation.

The same model picker is available while voice is paused. Changing from ChatGPT to OpenRouter uses the normal conversation, model, thinking, permissions, project context, and persistence path. Provider failures pause voice; they do not trigger an automatic provider switch.

## Settings and local processing

Settings → Voice and the voice pane provide microphone selection, installed Windows reply voices, speaking rate, pause duration, sensitivity, and read-aloud controls. Preferences belong to the signed-in Agentopia account and are saved in Windows-protected application storage. A missing selected microphone or voice produces an error so it can be reselected.

An AudioWorklet captures microphone frames without running the audio processor on the interface thread. The worklet is bundled as an application asset and loaded under the existing content security policy. Whisper still transcribes English inside the dedicated local WSL runtime. Windows SpeechSynthesizer generates WAV data in memory, and the app plays it through its audio context, providing one playback path that can be interrupted. Device echo cancellation is requested; a playback-transcript filter also suppresses substantial matching speech. Headphones help with physical speaker-to-microphone leakage.

Cancellation signals the actual local Whisper worker, waits for its exit, and removes the Windows and Linux audio files. Startup also removes audio left by an interrupted prior session. Account changes and application shutdown stop native voice work. Text from a finished transcription or generated audio is returned only if the original account remains signed in.

Only text is sent through the configured AI connection. ChatGPT authorization is used for its supported text inference path. This implementation does not use an audio endpoint under that authorization.
